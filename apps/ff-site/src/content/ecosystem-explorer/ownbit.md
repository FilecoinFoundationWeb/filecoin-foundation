Ownbit supports native FIL in a self-custody wallet for iPhone and Android. Users manage their own keys and use the app to receive and send FIL. The Filecoin integration gives users access to FIL alongside their other supported assets in a mobile wallet. Ownbit is an end-user wallet rather than a storage provider or IPFS hosting service. The complete application is proprietary and uses paid membership. Current product information, membership terms and official download links are available at https://ownbit.io/en/.
